#### Data anonymization steps

- [Add noise to date](wrangler-step-add-noise-to-date.md): replace the date with new random date near the original value to anonymize your date values.
- [Add noise to number](wrangler-step-add-noise-to-number.md): replace the original value with newly generated number that is near the original to anonymize numeric data.
- [Mask text](wrangler-step-mask-text.md): mask parts of text to hide personal or private data.
